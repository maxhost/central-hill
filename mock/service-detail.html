About this experience

The spiritual heart of Portugal, in one unhurried day

A day trip to the spiritual heart of Portugal: the Sanctuary of Fátima, the Gothic Monastery of Batalha, the record-breaking waves of Nazaré, and the whitewashed medieval walls of Óbidos.

What you'll do

The day, step by step

  1. 1
    08:30

    Pickup

    Your driver-guide meets you at the apartment.

  2. 08:30 – 12:00

    Fátima

    Free time at the Sanctuary and the Basilica of the Most Holy Trinity.

  3. 12:00 – 14:00

    Batalha

    Visit the Monastery of Batalha and stop for lunch (not included).

  4. 14:00 – 15:00

    Nazaré

    See the Guinness World Record waves from the clifftop.

  5. 15:00 – 16:30

    Óbidos

    Wander the medieval walled village.

  6. 6
    16:30 – 17:30

    Return

    Back in Lisbon by early evening.

About this service

An evening to yourselves, without the guesswork

Our sitters are background-checked, first-aid trained and speak English; many also speak French or Spanish. They come to the apartment, follow your routine for bath and bedtime, and send a message when the children are asleep.

Book a single evening or a recurring slot across a longer stay. Daytime cover is available too, which families with small children usually discover on day two and book for the rest of the week.
